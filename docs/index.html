The Website Experience, Step by Step

Hosted completely offline at http://192.168.4.1 (SSID: Restroom-Monitor) directly from ESP8266 flash memory.

01 · Boot & Calibration

Animated Circuit Loading Screen

Upon connecting to the gateway, an animated SVG schematic visualizes the UART link between the ATmega328P and ESP8266 across the 1 kΩ / 2.2 kΩ divider.

  • 4-Stage Boot Sequence: Gateway link, UART handshake, MicroSD history restoration, and UI preparation.
  • Guided Calibration & Rule Config: Captures clean-air MQ-135 baseline, dry/wet tank span, zero-flow baseline, and adaptive cleaning limits.
02 · Main Home Screen

100 / 100 Score & Dynamic Fault/Air Alerts

The Home screen centers around the 0–100 Cleanliness Ring and adapts automatically to live sensor states—from 100 / 100 FRESH to 35 / 100 Due now or probe/air fault alerts.

  • Live Status Strip & Alerts: Shows ATmega link, MicroSD logging, DS3231 RTC clock, Fan mode, Door state, and diagnostic banners.
  • One-Tap Cleaning Reset: Records cleaning timestamps, before/after air index, and resets the cleanliness counter.
03 · Tactile Sensor Cards

Air, Fan, Tank, Flow & Door Visualizers

Every sensor expands into a crisp card with real-time animations and calibrated readouts.

  • Air & Fan: 270° particle gauge (143 GOOD), 10-minute air trend slope, and AUTO / MANUAL relay control.
  • Water & Door: Wave tank level (100% WET), tap flow animation (3.09 L/min active vs. 0.00 L/min idle), and HC-SR04 door cycle counter.

Edge Analytics, Daily Report & Insights (Core Objective)

Computed in the browser from 288 five-minute buckets and MicroSD logs—delivering correlations, peak traffic heatmaps, water-per-entry metrics, cleaning audits, and printable daily reports.

Entries vs. Air Index Correlation

Evaluates Pearson correlation (r) across 0–15 minute lags to quantify how entries affect air quality and support adaptive cleaning intervals.

Weekday × Hour Traffic Heatmap

7×24 occupancy matrix identifying peak hours (e.g., Fri 16:00) and total recorded entries.

Water Per Entry & Leak Detection

Tracks litres per entry (0.46 L/entry) and detects sustained flow when no entries occur.

Exhaust Fan Effectiveness

Compares 5-minute air index change with the exhaust fan ON vs. OFF and reports 24-hour fan duty cycle.

Before & After Cleaning Audit

Logs cleaning events with entries since last clean and relative air index change 5 minutes after cleaning.

Printable Restroom Daily Report

Generates a clean summary report covering entries, peak hour, air avg/max, water used, fan duty, cleanliness score, and cleans today.

UART v2 Reliable Framing Protocol

Every telemetry frame at 9600 baud includes a sequence number and CRC-8 (polynomial 0x07), with ESP8266 PACKET_ACK and UNO retry logic.

T,2,<seq>,<YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS>,<airRaw>,<airStatus>,<people>,<waterRaw>,<waterAvail>,<waterPct>,<distCm>,<flowLpm>,<totalL>,<fanOn>,<mode>,<rtcOK>,<faultBits>,<faultText>,<CRC8>
